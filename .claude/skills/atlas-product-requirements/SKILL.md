# Atlas Product Requirements

1. Read README.md, AGENTS.md and the locked working-backwards plan.
2. State the user, context, problem, desired outcome and evidence for the proposed capability.
3. Map the journey from entry point through success, empty/loading/error/partial success/retry and recovery.
4. Define functional and non-functional acceptance criteria, including privacy, accessibility, responsive behavior, ownership, portability and source provenance where relevant.
5. Separate MVP requirements from later hypotheses; explain value, complexity, dependencies and what is deferred.
6. Use evidence from real workflows and existing systems. Do not treat prototype controls or feature presence as proof of usability.
7. Define how success will be measured and what evidence would falsify the proposal.
8. Record open questions and owner decisions; do not invent product requirements to fill a template.
9. Produce a reviewable requirements proposal. Do not approve architecture or start production implementation without the owner gate.
