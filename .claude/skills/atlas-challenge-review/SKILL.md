# Atlas Challenge Review

1. Read the original requirements and competing proposals independently.
2. Identify the strongest argument against each proposal, including your own initial preference.
3. Separate factual disagreements, assumptions, value trade-offs and unknowns.
4. Check primary sources and request a reproducible experiment for high-impact uncertain claims.
5. Challenge complexity, security, privacy, portability, failure recovery, cost and lock-in.
6. Do not use majority vote as a decision rule; compare evidence and consequences.
7. Preserve minority opinions and unresolved questions in the synthesis.
8. Recommend approve, revise, experiment or defer, and identify the human approval gate.
