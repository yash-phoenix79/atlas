Atlas / research provenance / 05

Every claim points back
to inspectable evidence.

Conceptual evidence model for research drafts. Separate resources, locators, claims, and the link between a claim and each supporting or conflicting source. This is a domain hypothesis, not a deployed schema.

Claim/source is many-to-many

ClaimSourceLink records the exact relationship; support and contradiction should not be inferred from color or proximity.

Locator is part of provenance

A citation should lead to a resource and a page, section, timestamp, or character range where possible.

Verification is a separate state

Generation, successful link resolution, source support, and human review are different events.

Open decisions: quote storage/copyright rules, version drift, citation formats, verification methodology, conflict resolution, and retention of model prompts/outputs require policy and schema review.
